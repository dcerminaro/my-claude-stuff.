# Lesson VI: Reserve (build an emergency fund)

Video: `out/lesson-06.mp4` (composition `Lesson06`)

## Caption

At Pharsalus I kept soldiers in reserve. When Pompey's cavalry charged, my reserve crushed them. ⚔️🛡️

Lesson VI: your emergency fund is your reserve. Keep about 3 to 6 months of essential expenses in cash, somewhere safe and easy to reach. When the car breaks or the job disappears, you won't be forced to sell investments at a loss or live on high-interest credit cards.

Build your reserve before you charge into battle, you reckless barbarians. 🏛️

Follow for Lesson VII. Caesar is NOT done with you. 📜
